# Quaternius Medieval Weapons

Models from the [LowPoly Medieval Weapons pack](https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalweapons.html)
by Quaternius (September 2018 release, also on
[OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/node/88080)), released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain). Only the OBJ/MTL files that `tools/art/uniques.py`
uses are kept: swords, daggers, the claymore, axes, hammers and bows.

`uniques.py` imports them in Blender, swaps their flat colours for our
materials (the item's metal, tinted steel, grip and stone) and stretches
each a little, so every unique weapon gets its own picture.
