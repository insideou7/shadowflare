ShadowFlare

An open reimplementation of the 2001 action RPG, running in your browser.

Play the open game: our own maps, art, sound and story.

Play the original with your own copy of ShadowFlare. Pick the game's folder (the one with Map and System in it, or any folder above it).

Your files stay on this computer. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Send crash reports to help us fix bugs?

If you say yes, the game's errors and your browser and system type are sent to Sentry (a crash reporting service) when something breaks. Your saves, your ShadowFlare files and personal details are never sent, and nothing is sent unless you say yes. You can turn this off here at any time.
