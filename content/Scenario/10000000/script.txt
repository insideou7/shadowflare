# Remote Town's people: where every new game begins, as in the original
# (scenario 0). The talk follows the original's order, purposes and
# flags in our own words:
#
# - the commander greets the hero over several boxes and hands over the
#   army's kit and a little gold (save:4 met him); below level 5 he has
#   nothing more; from level 5, once the beast to the north-east is
#   dealt with (quest:0 cleared), he sends the hero to clear the Dusty
#   Ruins (quest:3); when that is done he pays 1500 gold (save:2) and
#   sends the hero west through the Wasteland of Pillars to Cold Svalt.
# - the smith keeps the shop: sour (save:9 heard his complaint) until the
#   beast is dead, then warms up (save:6 1, then 2 once he says so) and
#   asks for his stolen gem back (quest:1); for it he tells of an exiled
#   trader in the Wasteland of Pillars. Once the ruins are clear and the
#   commander has sent the hero on, he gives a parting gift (save:8); he
#   hands over the old knight's order for quest:5 (save:21); once the
#   first area is finished (save:71) he sells only potions (save:70).
#   Otherwise he trades, identifies and repairs.
# - the healer gives the first mission (quest:0), later the shrine stone
#   (quest:2, once quest:3 is taken and the hero is past level 5), thanks
#   the hero for it and gives a parting gift (save:7); otherwise she heals
#   the hero when hurt.
#
# Each person keeps which of their boxes is showing in a TEMP flag (the
# commander temp:2, the smith temp:11, the healer temp:21) so that
# closing it shows the next. As IF runs its sentence and carries on, each
# talk-end sentence first copies that flag, and each check comes before
# the sentence that would make it true.
#
# The warp gate north-east of the square, as the original's: walking up
# to it finds it (warp:0, which a new game already has) and shows its
# name over it, and a click on it opens the GATE window.

object=9361,2086,9658,2337,gate
# The ground round it is a judge area only: unusable, as the original's, so
# a held click on the square still walks.
object=8160,697,10460,3197,gate-ground,states=0/0/0
